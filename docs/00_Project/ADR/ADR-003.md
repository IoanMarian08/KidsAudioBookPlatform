> **Historical decision — superseded.** This short early ADR is retained only as decision history. The active PostgreSQL design is [ADR-0002-postgresql-primary-system-of-record.md](ADR-0002-postgresql-primary-system-of-record.md). Modern ADR-0002 is the normative decision for PostgreSQL ownership, boundaries and consistency. Do not implement from this legacy record.


## Context

The platform has structured data: users, child profiles, stories, subscriptions, notifications, audit logs and content metadata.

---

## Decision

Use PostgreSQL as the primary relational database.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- MySQL
- MongoDB
- PostgreSQL
- Firebase

---

## Consequences

- Strong relational consistency
- Good indexing
- Mature ecosystem
- Works well with Spring Data JPA

---

## Notes

This decision may be revisited if product scale, team size or business constraints change.
