---

> **Historical decision — superseded.** This short early ADR is retained only as decision history. The active Modular monolith design is [ADR-0001-modular-monolith-first.md](ADR-0001-modular-monolith-first.md). The early proposal for immediate microservices was replaced by an initial modular monolith with bounded contexts and asynchronous workers. Microservices remain an evidence-based future option, not the MVP deployment default. Do not implement from this legacy record.


## Context

The platform needs authentication, content management, media, subscriptions, ads, notifications and analytics. These areas may scale independently over time.

---

## Decision

Use Spring Boot for backend services and organize the backend as disciplined microservices.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- Single monolith
- Modular monolith
- Spring Boot microservices
- Node.js services

---

## Consequences

- Clear service boundaries
- Scalable architecture
- More operational complexity
- Requires Docker and service communication standards

---

## Notes

This decision may be revisited if product scale, team size or business constraints change.
