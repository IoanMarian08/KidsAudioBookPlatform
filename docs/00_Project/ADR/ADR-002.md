---

> **Historical decision — superseded as an artifact.** The product owner explicitly chose independently deployable microservices again on 2026-10-10, but the **current and complete authoritative decision is [ADR-0015](ADR-0015-microservices-from-first-release.md)**, not this early brief record. The earlier modular-monolith ADR-0001 is now superseded. Consult ADR-0015 for service boundaries, isolated databases, REST/RabbitMQ contracts and implementation gates.


## Context

The platform needs authentication, content management, media, subscriptions, ads, notifications and analytics. These areas may scale independently over time.

---

## Decision

Use Spring Boot for backend services and organize the backend as disciplined microservices.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- Single monolith
- Modular monolith
- Spring Boot microservices
- Node.js services

---

## Consequences

- Clear service boundaries
- Scalable architecture
- More operational complexity
- Requires Docker and service communication standards

---

## Notes

This decision may be revisited if product scale, team size or business constraints change.
