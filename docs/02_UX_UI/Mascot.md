# Mascot: Rabbit Character Guidelines

Version: 2.0.0  
Status: Character brief; official name to be decided  
Owner: Product and Creative Direction

## 1. Canonical identity

**Species: rabbit.** The Product Bible names a rabbit mascot and does not select a final name. Names such as Lumi, Nori, Pufi, Luno, Tupi or Bubu are *candidates only*. Do not hardcode a candidate name in app strings, analytics or product copy before approval.

Character qualities: calm, warm, expressive, curious, inclusive, comforting and connected to bedtime and storytelling. It acts as a quiet companion rather than a sales representative.

## 2. Appropriate appearances

| Context | Role | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Briefly explain where stories live | Adult controls remain clearly adult-only |
| Child home | Recognizable reassuring presence | Not blocking content/navigation |
| Empty state | Gentle supportive illustration | Provide actionable text alternative |
| Bedtime mode | Quiet resting/sleeping pose | No sudden sound or movement |
| Story completion | Soft encouragement | No addictive streak/reward pressure |
| Offline state | Explain that downloaded stories still work | Never falsely claim content is available |

## 3. Prohibited behaviors

- Asking children to purchase, subscribe, view ads or pressure parents for money.
- Emotional coercion: disappointment for not opening the app, fake urgency or guilt.
- Promoting endless listening or sleep disruption.
- Implying the character is a real person/therapist or knows private facts about the child.
- Speaking to children through unrestricted generative AI.

## 4. Design and animation

Use digital-watercolor shapes and the approved palette, consistent ear/eye proportions and silhouette. Expressions must be legible without extreme facial drama. Pose library: hello, listening, reading, encouraging, sleepy, gentle error/help. Each should have a static fallback. Motion follows [Animations](Animations.md), reduced-motion settings and low-power mode.

## 5. Voice, copy and localization

Copy must be short, concrete and free of commercial pressure. Examples:
- "Let's choose a story."
- "Your story is ready."
- "We can try again when the internet is back."
- "Time for a quiet story."

Localized strings should adapt naturally; do not force mascot rhymes or invented words that translate poorly. Voice narration requires the same safety/rights review as story audio.

## 6. Asset package and governance

Every mascot release needs versioned vector/raster sources, poses, light/night variants, transparent backgrounds, alt text, usage examples and owner approval. Do not ship unlicensed third-party mascot art. Changing species or personality requires Product Bible revision and product decision.
